Vanity key generator

Create a MeshCore identity whose public key starts with a prefix you choose. Keys are made in your browser and are never sent anywhere. Back to the flasher

A 1-byte prefix (2 characters) is how repeaters appear in paths; pick one nobody nearby uses. 00 and FF are reserved and rejected.
More than 5 characters can take hours to days, and 7 to 8 is impractical in a browser. Expect a very long wait.
